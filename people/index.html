Rodolfo Ostilla Mónico

I lead the group. I am an Associate Professor (Profesor Titular) of Fluid Mechanics at the University of Cádiz, where I work on the numerical simulation of turbulent flows. Our research covers sustainable ventilation and thermal convection, the hydrodynamics of musical instruments, and shear and vortex flows.

I trained as an aerospace engineer in Seville and completed my PhD in the Physics of Fluids at the University of Twente in 2015. I then worked as a postdoctoral researcher at Harvard University and as an assistant professor at the University of Houston, before joining the University of Cádiz in 2022 with an Emergia fellowship.

Current members

Doctoral students (Universidad de Cádiz)

Gabriel A. Tarditti is a PhD student focusing on the fluid dynamics of ventilation. His research combines direct numerical simulation (DNS) of airflow in rooms with the evaluation of ventilation performance in real buildings through CO₂ measurements. This builds on earlier CFD work on the aerodynamics of a stratospheric airship. He complements this research profile with industrial experience in aircraft structural design, developing retrofit modifications in metallic and composite structures from concept through installation.

Thesis: Design of wind towers for passive cooling (expected 2027).

Ammara Tassawar is a PhD student at the University of Cádiz working on the hydrodynamics and aeroacoustics of flue-like wind instruments such as the recorder. She uses direct numerical simulations with an immersed boundary method to study how the jet–labium interaction in the mouthpiece produces self-sustained oscillations. She holds an MS in Applied Mathematics from COMSATS University Islamabad.

Thesis: Hydrodynamic instabilities in flue-like instruments (expected 2028).
